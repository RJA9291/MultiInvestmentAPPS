# 01. Product Vision - MultiInvestmentAPPS (AIOS)

## Vision Statement

To give everyday investors an AI co-pilot that understands their full financial picture across multiple investment types, and helps them make clearer, more confident decisions.

## Mission

Build a unified, AI-assisted platform (AIOS) where users can track, analyze, and plan multiple types of investments in one place, with insights generated from their own data and curated knowledge, not generic advice.

## Problem Statement

- Investors juggle multiple platforms (stocks, crypto, unit trust, property, business equity) with no single view.
- Existing apps show numbers but do not explain what they mean or what to do next.
- Good financial knowledge is scattered across documents, notes, and articles that are never connected to the user's actual portfolio.

## Target Users

- Retail investors managing more than one asset class.
- Individuals who want AI-assisted analysis without needing to be a financial expert.
- Small business owners/investors who track personal and business investments together.

## Value Proposition

- One dashboard for all investment types.
- AI-generated insights grounded in the user's own documents and data (see 08_ONEDRIVE_KNOWLEDGE_ENGINE.md).
- Actionable, explainable recommendations instead of raw numbers.

## Success Metrics (draft, to be refined)

- Time-to-insight: how fast a user understands portfolio health after login.
- Retention: weekly active usage of the dashboard.
- Trust: percentage of AI insights marked as "useful" by users.

## Non-Goals

- This platform does not execute trades or move funds on the user's behalf.
- This platform does not provide licensed financial advice; all AI output is informational.
